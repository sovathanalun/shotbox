Frequently asked questions

Why does Shotbox need access to my photos?

To find your screenshots and read the text in them. It only scans images marked as screenshots, plus photos you import yourself. Everything is recognized on your device.

What happens when I delete a screenshot in Shotbox?

iOS asks you to confirm, and the image goes to Recently Deleted in Photos. Shotbox keeps the text and a small preview under Saved Text.

A screenshot is in the wrong folder.

Open it, choose the folder menu and move it. Your choice is kept when Shotbox re-scans.

Search finds nothing.

Indexing reads the newest screenshots first and shows progress at the top of the Library. Allow full photo access in iOS Settings, then reopen the app.

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. See the Privacy Policy.
