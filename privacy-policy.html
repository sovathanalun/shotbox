Shotbox Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Your screenshots stay on your device

Shotbox reads the text in your screenshots so you can search them and sort them into folders. Text is recognized on your iPhone or iPad with Apple's on-device Vision framework. Screenshots, recognized text, notes and tags are stored only on your device. We do not run servers, and we never receive, read or sell your screenshots or their text.

Photos access

Shotbox asks for access to your photo library to find screenshots and read their text. It only scans images marked as screenshots, plus any images you import yourself. If you choose to delete a screenshot from Shotbox, iOS asks you to confirm, and the image goes to Recently Deleted in Photos. You can change or revoke access at any time in the Settings app.

Widgets

The Shotbox widget shows only counts (for example, how many receipts you have). No screenshot text is shared with the widget.

Advertising

Shotbox is free thanks to ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policy. Where required, you're asked for consent first, and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt lets you decline tracking. Your screenshots and their text are never shared with advertisers.

Backups and deletion

The Shotbox index is included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups if you have those turned on. Deleting the app removes its index from the device; your screenshots stay in Photos.

Children

Shotbox is not directed at children under 13.
